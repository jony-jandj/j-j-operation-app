# Bills and Job Closeout

Based on the local Summary Back Button release dated September 22.

- Renamed Subs & Materials Summary to Subs bills and In-house Summary to In-house bills.
- Renamed Work & Pay to Job Closeout in navigation, headings and messages.
- Added Mark Incomplete at the bottom-right of both Bills P.O. detail dialogs.
- Returns reopened work to Job Closeout with the correct job and team selected.
- Reopened subcontractor work is excluded from Subs bills and its print totals until completed again.
- Keeps approved P.O. allocations, original work snapshots and history. Recorded payments must be corrected before reopening.
- Advances the offline cache version. No database migration required.

This package has not been published to GitHub or the live site.
