P.O. Partial Completion and Cloud Saving — November 11, 2026

Upload these three files to the root of the existing j-j-operation-app GitHub repository, replacing the matching files:
  index.html
  work-pay.js
  service-worker.js

No SQL/database setup is required. No payment or project data is included in this ZIP.

After GitHub Pages finishes updating, reload the app. If an older screen persists, close and reopen the app.

Partially Complete means send completed work to Bills, not record payment:
- Enter the total completed percentage (50%, then 75% sends only the additional 25%).
- The P.O. stays in Job Closeout, showing Sent to Bills and Remaining to send to Bills.
- Bills shows only the portion sent there. Record the actual payment in Bills.
- Paid, Due in Bills, and Total left to pay remain separate.
- Fully completed tickets with a mixture of fully paid and unpaid crew members remain visible as partially paid.

Cloud saving:
- Cloud sign-in, reads, writes, and polling have bounded waits so a stalled connection cannot hold the save lock forever.
- Existing local data, merge safeguards, revision checks, and retry behavior are preserved.
- Transient load errors no longer incorrectly instruct you to run setup SQL.

Validated with local browser fixtures and simulated cloud failures. Live connectivity and your real records were not modified or verified. This ZIP is ready for upload; it has not been deployed.
