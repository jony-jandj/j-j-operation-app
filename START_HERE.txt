AUTOMATIC PAY PERIODS

Upload work-pay.js to the root of jony-jandj/j-j-operation-app on GitHub. Commit, wait for deployment, then refresh the app (Mac Chrome: Command + Shift + R). No SQL update is needed.

When recording payment:
1. Select unpaid work for one employee or sub.
2. Review automatically assigned pay periods based on the completed dates. The remaining balance is already filled in.
3. Confirm the actual payment date, method, amount and any reference, then Save payment record.

Multiple pay periods are saved together, with one history record for each period. They share the actual payment date, method and reference. Subtotals are shown before saving.

Use Change pay period for selected work only if you need a manual exception. It places all selected lines into your chosen period. Missing completed dates require correction or an explicit manual period. Corrected completed dates are checked again before saving. Existing historical payments are not reassigned or rewritten.

The two-week Sunday cycle still uses September 6, 2026 as its reference and extends backward. Payment date remains separate from work period. This records payments already made; it does not send money.
